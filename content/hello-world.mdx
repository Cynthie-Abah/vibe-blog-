# Hello World

This is the first post on my new blog. After years of writing on platforms I do not control, I finally decided to build something of my own. Here, I can choose the typography, the layout, the performance budget, and the publishing workflow. Every pixel is intentional.

## Why I started writing

Writing is how I think. When I force an idea into sentences, the gaps in my understanding become visible. A blog turns that process into a public record. It is not about being an expert; it is about learning in the open.

Some topics I plan to cover:

- Front-end engineering and React patterns
- Performance and accessibility
- Developer experience and tooling
- Lessons from building side projects

## A quote I keep returning to

> "The best time to plant a tree was twenty years ago. The second best time is now."

The same is true for starting a blog. There is no perfect first post. The only mistake is waiting until you feel ready.

## What comes next

I will publish short, focused articles that respect the reader's time. Each post will include:

1. A clear problem or idea
2. Practical examples where possible
3. A concise takeaway

If you want to follow along, subscribe to the newsletter or grab the RSS feed.
